# Quickstart

Your first five minutes with Kayne.

1. **Ask him a question.** Any question. He won't make you feel dumb.
2. **Watch him explain it.** Notice how he asks you questions back. That's the feature, not a bug.
3. **Try it yourself.** He'll let you drive.
4. **Get stuck.** He'll nudge, not take over.
5. **Figure it out.** He'll act like you did something amazing. You did.

{: .note }
Repeat as needed. Kayne has no rate limit.
